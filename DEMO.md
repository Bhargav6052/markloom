Use real verified accounts and an isolated test organization clearly named as a test. Fixture-only PGlite tests already cover the central transaction journey; they are not live AI, email or storage evidence.

1. Open landing, exercise motion control/mobile fallback, then sign up and confirm email.
2. Create organization; invite a separate Creator and independent Manager/Reviewer. Sign in separately and verify role denial and second-organization isolation.
3. Admin reviews Privacy Center and sets permitted PUBLIC/INTERNAL classes. Keep confidential/restricted blocked.
4. Upload a real source PDF/DOCX with an appropriate classification, create exact quoted source/fact versions and explicitly activate them.
5. Creator saves classified text and uploads/reopens a real original file; save immutable revision and inspect hash/provenance.
6. Inspect privacy preflight including source/document classes. Run local checks and inspect the limited-coverage label. With reviewed credentials/policy, explicitly opt in to one real provider call and inspect its recorded attempt.
7. Inspect findings and exact citations, accept/edit/dismiss a correction, save a new immutable version and rerun analysis.
8. Submit saved version. Independent assigned specialist/manager comments and decides; verify the owner/AI cannot approve. Inspect exact-version passport/hash/evidence snapshot.
9. Admin creates a changed fact version, previews exact dependent content, activates it and checks impacted content, notification and preserved historical approval.
10. Revise and reapprove. Inspect real analytics, audit, Privacy Center attempts and data requests. Check no fabricated user activity or statistics.

Three-minute narration: establish approved evidence → check a draft with privacy controls → show independent human passport → change a fact and follow exact impact → inspect processing history. If provider setup is absent, explicitly demonstrate local-only coverage and state the live AI step is blocked; never replace it with canned output.
